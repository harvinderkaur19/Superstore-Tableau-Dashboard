# Superstore Tableau Dashboard


## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales, profit, shipping, and regional performance using Tableau dashboards. The dataset contains information related to orders, sales, profit, shipping modes, customer segments, regions, states, and product sub-categories.

The objective of this project is to identify business trends, profitable regions, high-performing product categories, and operational insights through interactive visualizations.

---

# Tools & Technologies Used

* Tableau
* Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset
* Data Visualization Techniques
* Business Analytics

---

# Dataset Features

The dataset includes:

* Order Date
* Sales
* Profit
* Region
* State
* Segment
* Ship Mode
* Category
* Sub-Category
* Quantity

---

# Dashboard 1: Business Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

This dashboard focuses on:

* Regional profitability
* Monthly sales trends
* Segment-wise sales performance
* Sub-category profitability analysis

---

## Key Insights

### Region and Sub-Category Wise Profit

* The West region generates the highest profit across most sub-categories.
* Copiers, Phones, and Accessories are highly profitable products.
* Tables and Bookcases generate losses in several regions.

### Year and Month Wise Sales

* Sales show a strong upward trend from 2019 to 2023.
* Significant sales spikes are visible during late 2022 and early 2023.
* The highest monthly sales exceed 118K.

### Region and Segment Wise Sales

* The Consumer segment contributes the highest sales in every region.
* The West region records the highest overall sales.
* The South region contributes the least sales.

# Dashboard 2: Retail Performance Analytics Dashboard

## Dashboard Overview

This dashboard focuses on:

* Profitability analysis
* Sales-profit relationships
* Shipping insights
* State-wise performance
* Regional contribution analysis

---

## Key Insights

### Sales vs Profit Correlation

* Sales and profit have a positive relationship.
* Some high-sales transactions generate low or negative profits.

### Segment-wise Sales High vs Low

* Consumer segment consistently records the highest sales quantity.
* Sales increase steadily over time across all segments.

### State-wise Sales Analysis

* California is the highest-performing state in terms of sales.
* Sales are concentrated mainly in western and coastal states.

### Subcategory Sales Analysis

* Chairs, Phones, Copiers, and Storage generate strong sales.
* Some sub-categories show fluctuating sales patterns.

### 2020 Regional Contribution %

* The West region contributes the highest percentage of total sales.
* The South region contributes the least.

### Average Profit by Ship Mode

* First Class shipping generates the highest average profit.
* Standard Class has the lowest average profitability.

---

# Overall Business Conclusion

* The business demonstrates strong sales and profit growth over time.
* The Consumer segment and West region are the primary revenue drivers.
* Certain products generate losses despite strong sales, indicating pricing or discount-related issues.
* Expanding into lower-performing regions and optimizing shipping strategies can improve profitability further.
